Sales Advisor

Join the team helping small businesses get online — fast. Your instinct, your persistence, your earnings.

Full-Time Remote / Field careers@velasync.com Commission-Only · Uncapped
At Vela Sync, we help small and growing businesses get online quickly with professionally designed websites. As a Sales Advisor, you'll be the first voice a potential customer hears — and the reason they say yes. This is a high-impact, people-first role where your instinct, warmth, and persistence directly determine your success.
Overview

About the Role

You'll work a curated lead list provided by Vela Sync, reaching out to business owners who are ready to take the next step online. Armed with a proven calling script and your own natural sales intuition, your job is to guide each lead through a confident, consultative conversation — and close with a website solution they're excited about.

You won't be cold-calling in the dark. Every lead is pre-qualified, and you'll have all the tools you need from day one: the list, the script, and a blank website template to share with prospects so they can immediately visualise their future site.

How You'll Work

Your Day-to-Day Process

Work the Lead List

Each day you'll receive an up-to-date lead list from Vela Sync — real business owners who have already shown interest. No manual prospecting required.

Make the Call

Use the Vela Sync calling script as your foundation. It's there to keep you on track, but your tone, timing, and personality are what turn a script into a genuine conversation.

Sell with Confidence

Listen to the lead's needs, overcome objections naturally, and present the Vela Sync website package as the clear solution. You're not pushing a product — you're solving a real problem.

Share the Template

Once a lead is ready to move forward, provide them with the blank website template Vela Sync has prepared. This gives them an immediate, tangible next step — and keeps momentum high.

Log & Follow Up

Record call outcomes accurately and follow up on warm leads. Consistent pipeline management is what separates good months from great ones.

Chase Payment

Once a customer's website has been fully built and delivered, you'll follow up to ensure payment is collected. This means reaching out promptly, handling any queries professionally, and escalating where necessary — closing the loop on every sale you've made.

The Job

Key Responsibilities

Minimum targets: 500 calls per week · 5 sales per week minimum. Every sale beyond that is yours to keep building on.

  • Contact prospects from the Vela Sync lead list in a timely, organised manner
  • Deliver engaging, consultative sales calls using the provided script as a guide
  • Adapt your approach based on the lead's tone, industry, and specific needs
  • Present Vela Sync's website offering clearly and compellingly
  • Provide prospects with the blank website template at the appropriate point in the conversation
  • Handle objections professionally and with empathy
  • Accurately record call notes, outcomes, and follow-up actions
  • Chase outstanding payments from customers who have received their completed website build
  • Handle payment queries professionally and escalate unresolved cases where appropriate
  • Communicate any lead list feedback or market insights to the team
What We're Looking For

Requirements & Skills

  • Proven experience in a phone-based or direct sales role
  • Strong verbal communication — clear, confident, and personable
  • Natural sales instinct with the ability to read a conversation and adapt
  • Comfortable working with a script while still sounding human and unscripted
  • High level of self-motivation and the discipline to manage your own call targets
  • Organised approach to pipeline and follow-up management
  • Basic digital literacy — comfortable sharing and explaining a website template
  • A genuine interest in helping small businesses grow online
  • Resilience — you understand that rejection is part of the process, not the end of it
Bonus Points

Nice to Have

  • Background in web, digital, or tech product sales
  • Experience selling to SMEs or sole traders
  • Familiarity with CRM systems and call-logging tools
  • A second language — our lead lists span diverse business communities
