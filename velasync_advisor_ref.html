6-Stage Sales Pipeline

01
Onboarding
Know every package, price and KPI cold. Tools ready. Scripts memorised. Don't speak to a prospect until this is done.
›
02
Research
10 min before every meeting. Google them, check their site, review Google Maps score, audit 3 competitors. Walk in knowing their problem.
›
03
Discovery
Six questions in sequence. Listen deeply. Take notes visibly. Feed their answers back verbatim before you reveal anything.
›
04
Reveal & Pitch
Bridge their pain → open the draft site in silence → scroll slowly → anchor £2,000+ → land £450 or £650. Contrast does the selling.
›
05
Close
Acknowledge every objection. Reframe it as a question. Pick a close technique. Ask once, then stop talking. Silence is your ally.
›
06
Post-Close
Invoice same day. Log in CRM. Brief call booked before you leave. Build team notified. Site live within 24 hours. Close is not the finish line.

Scripts, Objections & Close Toolkit

Every word matters. Every silence is a weapon. Know this cold.
VS

6 Discovery Questions — In Order

1
Current State
"When a potential customer Googles you right now — what do they find?"
2
Awareness
"How are most of your customers finding you at the moment?"
3
Competition
"Have you looked at what your top three competitors are doing online?"
4
Revenue Impact
"76% of people research online before making contact — what does that mean for the customers you're missing?"
5
Previous Experience
"Have you ever tried to get a website built before? What happened?"
6
Future Vision ★ Most Powerful
"If your digital presence was exactly where you wanted it — what would change for your business?"
💡 After Q6: "So if I've understood you correctly…" — feed their answers back verbatim, word for word. Validates their concerns, amplifies the pain, sets up the pitch perfectly. Never skip this step.

Price Reveal Script

Step 1 — Anchor high: "A website of this quality from a traditional agency — you're looking at £2,000 minimum, often more."
Step 2 — Land low: "We charge £450. And you're live within 24 hours. Not a week. Not a month. Tomorrow."
Step 3 — Silence. Let the contrast land. The next person who speaks loses.

Objection Responses

"We already have a website."
Is it ranking? Is it bringing in enquiries — or is it just a digital brochure nobody reads?
"£450 seems too cheap."
You've just seen it. That is the quality. Enterprise engineers, zero agency overhead. That's how we do it.
"We need to think about it."
What specifically? Name the real objection. Then stop talking. Silence forces the answer.
"We want to get a few quotes first."
Equivalent quality starts at £2,000+. Come back and we will beat any genuine quote.
"We had a bad experience before."
"Tell me exactly what happened." Vela Sync was built specifically because of that gap in the market.
"Not sure about the monthly fee."
£50 a month is insurance on a £450 asset. It's the easiest financial decision in this room.

Close Techniques

Assumptive Close
"What's the domain name you're currently using?"
Assumes the sale — moves straight to logistics.
ROI Frame Close
"If the site brings in just one extra customer — what's that worth to you?"
Forces them to calculate the return in their own head.
Urgency Close
"Someone is Googling you right now. What is stopping us doing this today?" → silence.
Real urgency. Not manufactured. Then stop talking.
Option Close
"The £450 standard or the £650 SEO & Growth — which feels like the right fit?"
Both options are a yes. Choice replaces hesitation.
