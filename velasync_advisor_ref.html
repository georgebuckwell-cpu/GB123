The Complete Pipeline

Know every stage. Hit every beat. Close every room — zero ambiguity, one system.

6-Stage Sales Pipeline

01
Onboarding
Know every package, price & KPI cold. Tools ready. Don't speak to a prospect until complete.
›
02
Research
10 min before every meeting. Google, site check, Maps rating, 3 competitors. Walk in knowing their problem.
›
03
Discovery
6 questions in sequence. Listen deeply. Take notes visibly. Feed answers back verbatim before you reveal anything.
›
04
Reveal & Pitch
Bridge their pain → silent demo → anchor £2,000+ → land £450 or £650. Contrast does the selling.
›
05
Close
Acknowledge every objection. Reframe. Pick a close technique. Ask once — then stop talking.
›
06
Post-Close
Invoice same day. CRM logged. Brief call booked before you leave. Site live within 24 hours. Not finished yet.

Stage 02 — Pre-Meeting Research

1
Google Them
Search the business. Do they appear at all? What comes up first — them or a competitor?
2
Check Their Site
Is it mobile-friendly? Fast? Does it actually convert — or is it a liability?
3
Google Maps Rating
Low score = reputation problem. No reviews = visibility problem. You solve both.
4
Competitor Audit
Find their top 3 local competitors. Check online presence. Use it to create urgency.
Opening Line
"I had a look at your business before coming in today. I noticed a few things I wanted to talk about — but first, tell me how business has been. Are you getting as many new customers as you want?"
⛔ Never open with the product. Open with their world — not yours. The product comes third.

Scripts, Objections & Close Toolkit

Every word matters. Every silence is a weapon. Know this cold.

6 Discovery Questions

1
Current State
"When a potential customer Googles you right now — what do they find?"
2
Awareness
"How are most of your customers finding you at the moment?"
3
Competition
"Have you looked at what your top three competitors are doing online?"
4
Revenue Impact
"76% research online first — what does that mean for the customers you're missing?"
5
Previous Experience
"Have you ever tried to get a website built before? What happened?"
6
Future Vision ★
"If your digital presence was exactly where you wanted it — what would change?"
💡 After Q6: "So if I've understood you correctly…" — feed answers back verbatim. Then pivot to the reveal. Never skip this.

Price Reveal

1 Bridge the pain — summarise their answers. Make them feel heard.
2 Open draft site. Say nothing. Scroll slowly. Let them lean in.
3 "A traditional agency — £2,000 minimum. We charge £450. Live tomorrow."
4 Silence. The next person who speaks loses.

Objection Responses

"We already have a website."
Is it ranking? Is it bringing in enquiries — or a digital brochure nobody reads?
"£450 seems too cheap."
You've just seen it. That is the quality. Enterprise engineers, zero agency overhead.
"We need to think about it."
What specifically? Name the real objection. Then stop talking. Silence forces the answer.
"We want a few quotes first."
Equivalent quality starts at £2,000+. Come back — we will beat any genuine quote.
"We had a bad experience before."
"Tell me exactly what happened." Vela Sync was built to close that gap.
"Not sure about the monthly fee."
£50/mo is insurance on a £450 asset. Easiest decision in the room.

Power Phrases

"The question isn't whether — it's when."
"This isn't a cost. It's a decision."
"Every day you wait, your competitor doesn't."
"Based on everything you've told me, this is the obvious next step."

Close Techniques

Assumptive Close
"What's the domain name you're currently using?"
Assumes the sale. Moves straight to logistics.
ROI Frame Close
"If the site brings in one extra customer — what's that worth to you?"
Forces them to calculate the return in their own head.
Urgency Close
"Someone is Googling you right now. What's stopping us doing this today?" → silence.
Real urgency, not manufactured. Then stop talking.
Option Close
"The £450 standard or the £650 SEO & Growth — which feels right?"
Both options are a yes. Choice replaces hesitation.
Instalment Close
"Get it live tomorrow, pay on a schedule that suits you."
Removes the financial barrier without discounting.

Acknowledge · Reframe · Advance

Acknowledge: "I completely understand that."
Reframe: "What you're really asking is…"
Advance: Ask the close question. Then silence.
