Scripts, Objections & Close Toolkit

Every word matters. Every silence is a weapon. Know this cold.

6 Discovery Questions — In Order

1
Current State
"When a potential customer Googles you right now — what do they find?"
Forces them to confront their digital reality.
2
Awareness
"How are most of your customers finding you at the moment?"
Exposes over-reliance on referrals. One slow season away from a problem.
3
Competition
"Have you looked at what your top three competitors are doing online?"
Creates competitive anxiety. You already know the answer — make them say it.
4
Revenue Impact
"76% of people research online before contact — what does that mean for the customers you're missing?"
Forces them to calculate lost revenue in their own head.
5
Previous Experience
"Have you ever tried to get a website built before? What happened?"
Most have a war story. You are the antidote before you've mentioned the product.
6
Future Vision ★ Most Powerful
"If your digital presence was exactly where you wanted it — what would change for your business?"
Gets them to paint their own success picture out loud.
💡 After Q6: "So if I've understood you correctly…" — feed answers back verbatim. Validates concerns, amplifies the pain, sets up the pitch. Never skip this step.

Price Reveal Script

Step 1 — Bridge the pain. Summarise their answers in one clear statement. Make them feel heard.
Step 2 — Silent show. Open the draft site. Say nothing. Scroll slowly. When they lean in — that's your pivot.
Step 3 — Anchor high. "A website of this quality from a traditional agency — you're looking at £2,000 minimum."
Step 4 — Land low. "We charge £450. Live within 24 hours. Not a week. Not a month. Tomorrow."
Step 5 — Silence. The next person who speaks loses.

Objection Responses

"We already have a website."
Is it ranking? Is it bringing in enquiries — or is it a digital brochure nobody reads?
"£450 seems too cheap."
You've just seen it. That is the quality. Enterprise engineers, zero agency overhead. That's how.
"We need to think about it."
What specifically? Name the real objection. Then stop talking. Silence forces the answer.
"We want to get a few quotes first."
Equivalent quality starts at £2,000+. Come back — we will beat any genuine quote.
"We had a bad experience before."
"Tell me exactly what happened." Vela Sync was built specifically to close that gap.
"Not sure about the monthly fee."
£50 a month is insurance on a £450 asset. The easiest financial decision in the room.

Close Techniques

Assumptive Close
"What's the domain name you're currently using?"
Assumes the sale. Moves to logistics.
ROI Frame Close
"If the site brings in one extra customer — what's that worth to you?"
Forces them to calculate the return themselves.
Urgency Close
"Someone is Googling you right now. What's stopping us doing this today?" → silence.
Real urgency. Then stop talking.
Option Close
"The £450 standard or the £650 SEO & Growth — which feels right?"
Both options are a yes. Choice removes hesitation.
Instalment Close
"Get it live tomorrow, pay on a schedule that works for you. Removes the barrier entirely."
Removes the financial objection without discounting.
