We're hiring a Sales Advisor.

A remote, commission-only sales role for a driven closer who wants uncapped earnings, a warm lead list and a product that sells itself. Full-time. Start immediately.

At Vela Sync, we help small and growing businesses get online quickly with professionally designed websites. As a Sales Advisor, you'll be the first voice a potential customer hears, and the reason they say yes. Your warmth, instinct and persistence decide how well you do.

Role overview

Commission-only Sales Advisor, remote or field-based, full-time. You'll sell Vela Sync's website and digital packages directly to SME business owners across the UK. Pre-qualified leads provided from day one.

What you'll do

Make a minimum of 500 outbound calls per week from our pre-qualified lead list and close a minimum of 5 sales per week. Present and close Vela Sync packages (website builds, monthly support, marketing packs) directly to SME decision-makers.

Requirements

Proven track record in outbound or B2B sales. Confident on the phone with business owners. Self-motivated and results-driven. Comfortable working independently in a remote or field environment. Experience in digital or website sales is a bonus, not essential.

Compensation

100% commission with uncapped earning potential. Competitive rates per closed sale. Top performers targeting £2,000–£4,000+/month. Pre-qualified leads provided; no cold sourcing required.

Key responsibilities

  • Contact prospects from the Vela Sync lead list in a timely, organised manner
  • Deliver engaging, consultative sales calls using the provided script as a guide
  • Adapt your approach based on the lead's tone, industry and specific needs
  • Present Vela Sync's website offering clearly and compellingly
  • Handle objections professionally and with empathy
  • Accurately record call notes, outcomes and follow-up actions
  • Complete a minimum of 500 calls per week, with a minimum target of 5 sales
  • Chase outstanding payments once a customer's website has been delivered
