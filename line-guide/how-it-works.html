The matching algorithm

A score you can see through.

Every match gets a weighted fit score from four signals. We show the breakdown on each result so a recommendation is never a black box.

  • Use-case fit (45%) — does the supplier genuinely do this application, format and environment?
  • Verified rating (25%) — what real buyers reported after delivery.
  • Budget alignment (18%) — does the supplier operate in your capital band?
  • Delivery vs timeframe (12%) — can they hit your install window?
