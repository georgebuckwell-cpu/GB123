The challenge

Manual quality checks miss defects and cannot keep up at line speed. The challenge is reliable detection on variable natural products, integrated without slowing the line — and with auditable records.

What to look for

  • High detection accuracy on your real product variation
  • Inline operation at full line speed
  • Reject handling that does not jam the line
  • Auditable records for compliance and traceability
  • Easy reconfiguration for new products/labels

Questions to ask suppliers

01 What detection rate can you achieve on our product?
02 How are false rejects minimised?
03 How are inspection records logged for audits?
04 How quickly can we add a new product or label check?
