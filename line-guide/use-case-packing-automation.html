The challenge

Food & beverage packing lines juggle short runs, frequent changeovers and tight hygiene rules. The wrong system locks you into one format or chokes on variety. The right one flexes across SKUs without sacrificing speed.

What to look for

  • Fast, tool-less changeover between formats and SKUs
  • Hygienic, washdown-rated design where required
  • Proven throughput at your target line speed
  • Integration with upstream filling and downstream end-of-line
  • Strong service & spares support to protect uptime

Questions to ask suppliers

01 What is the realistic changeover time between our top SKUs?
02 Can you demonstrate the quoted speed on our product, not a sample?
03 What is the expected OEE and how is it supported?
04 How does the cell integrate with our existing line controls?
