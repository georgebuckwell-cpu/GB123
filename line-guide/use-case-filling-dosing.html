The challenge

Every product fills differently — viscosity, foaming, particulates and temperature all change the game. The challenge is accuracy across your range while staying hygienic and quick to clean down.

What to look for

  • Fill accuracy proven on your actual product
  • Hygienic, CIP-ready design for fast changeover
  • Recipe management for multiple products
  • Handling for your product type (liquid/powder/viscous)
  • Minimal giveaway to protect margin

Questions to ask suppliers

01 What fill accuracy can you guarantee on our product?
02 How long is a full clean-down and changeover?
03 How is recipe/format change managed and stored?
04 What is the expected product giveaway percentage?
