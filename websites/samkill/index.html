Where Dutch precision meets street-ready style.

SamKill Clothing was born from a simple belief — that menswear should be both uncompromising in quality and fearless in character. Founded in the Netherlands, every piece in our collection is built for the man who moves with intention, dresses with purpose, and refuses to blend in.

From lightweight padded jackets engineered for the European climate to bombers cut for the streets of Amsterdam, our garments sit at the intersection of continental tailoring and contemporary streetwear. Premium materials, considered construction, accessible price points. This is modern Dutch menswear.
