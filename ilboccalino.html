Our Story

A corner of Italy in the heart of Amsterdam

Nestled on the Utrechtsestraat, Il Boccalino has been welcoming guests with the warmth and passion of true Italian hospitality since 1995. Every dish is prepared with seasonal ingredients sourced directly from trusted Italian producers.

Our kitchen tells the story of Italy's regions — from the rich ragùs of Bologna to the delicate seafood of the Amalfi Coast. Sit back, breathe in the aroma of fresh herbs and wood-fire, and let Italy come to you.

The Experience

More than a meal — a memory

Step through our doors and the noise of Amsterdam fades. Candlelight, warm terracotta tones, the gentle murmur of Italian — Il Boccalino wraps you in an atmosphere that feels like a family trattoria in Tuscany.

Whether you're celebrating a milestone or simply savouring a mid-week escape, our team is devoted to making your evening exceptional.
