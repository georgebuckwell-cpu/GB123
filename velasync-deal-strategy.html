How VelaSync should structure — and exit — the Line Guide deal

A VelaSync-side view: which of the three structures maximises our risk-adjusted return, what to push for at the table, and how we get our money out if we ever need to.

The core insight

Recurring commission is an annuity. Equity is a lottery ticket.

The recurring fee — a percentage of all the revenue we generate, with no end date — is an income stream that compounds every quarter and never switches off. Equity only pays out on an exit that may be years away, may be small, or may never happen. For a services firm, the annuity is almost always the higher-value, lower-risk asset. The equity is cheap optionality on top — worth having, not worth trading the annuity for.

The one-line strategy

Maximise the recurring percentage, take a modest equity slice for exit upside, and make sure the recurring stream is protected on a change of control — because that protection is also our exit.

The recommendation

Anchor on the annuity, take equity as a kicker

What to push for

  • Open on Option A (35%, recurring, no end date) — it's our strongest position and the highest-EV outcome in every scenario short of a big, near exit. The “no end date” is the crown jewel; don't give it away cheaply.
  • Settle around a strong-recurring hybrid — concede from 35% toward ~25–30% only in exchange for a 5–10% equity kicker. That keeps most of the annuity and adds exit upside for very little given up.
  • Protect the stream on change of control — if Line Guide is sold, the recurring commission must convert to a buy-out multiple (3–4× annual run-rate). This is upside protection and our liquidity event.
  • Tie equity vesting to revenue we generate — milestones we control, not vague company goals.

Prefer A/hybrid if…

You expect the company to keep trading, or any exit to be years away or modest. This is the base case — bet on the annuity.

Lean to C if…

You have strong conviction in a near, large exit (>£18m) and are willing to trade guaranteed cash for that bet.

Never…

Give up the recurring “no end date” without a buy-out multiple, or accept equity without tag-along and vesting you control.

Getting out

How we exit if we need to

Walk from the work

End the consultancy on 3 months' notice, keeping a trailing commission tail (~12 months) on customers already won — or trigger the buy-out.

Sell the income

Have Line Guide buy out the recurring commission for a lump sum (3–4× annual run-rate) — a clean, pre-agreed cash exit that doesn't depend on a company sale.

Realise the equity

Tag-along on a company sale, sell in a secondary to incoming investors, or a buy-back at fair value under an agreed valuation mechanism.

Make the exit un-blockable

Get all three routes written into the agreements up front — notice + tail, a commission buy-out formula, and equity tag-along + a valuation mechanism. The point is that we never need Line Guide's goodwill to get our money out; the mechanics are pre-agreed on day one.
